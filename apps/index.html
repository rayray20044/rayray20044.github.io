Modulor Smart Locker Shopping App

01
  • My roleUI design & Flutter development
  • PlatformMobile app (Flutter)
  • ToolsFigma · Flutter · Dart
  • SpecialConnected to real lockers

The challenge

Shopping that ends at a locker, not a counter.

The brief: a complete shopping experience for students that ends at a physical smart locker instead of a shop counter. Browse supplies, add them to a cart, pay, then walk up to the locker and unlock your order with a QR code.

What I did

Designed the flow, then built it for real.

  • Designed the UI and the full purchase flow in Figma, from categories to order review
  • Built the app in Flutter and Dart on a working backend
  • Connected the app to the real locker hardware, so a QR code actually opens the door
The result

A working app, not just a mockup: the whole journey from browsing to pickup runs end to end on real lockers.

EDURINO In-Store Kids' Learning Game

02
  • ClientEDURINO × Müller stores
  • My roleScreen prototype, ad flow, demo & landing page
  • ToolsFigma · ProtoPie · HTML · CSS
  • Year2024

The challenge

Get kids to play, and parents to sign up.

EDURINO makes educational products for children and wanted to promote them on screens inside Müller stores. The screen had two audiences: kids, who need something fun enough to stop for, and parents, who need a quick reason to sign up.

What I did

Play first, sign-up second.

  • Designed the screen prototype and ad flow in Figma and ProtoPie
  • Used friendly characters, mini-games and certificates to make learning feel like play
  • Built a working demo and a landing page that turns parents' interest into sign-ups
The result

A clickable in-store concept and landing page built to present to a real client. See it in the 44-second demo above.

Nutrihero: Gamified Nutrition App

03
  • AudienceTeens who struggle at school
  • My roleApp design & landing page development
  • ToolsFigma · ProtoPie · HTML · CSS · JS
  • StatusLanding page live

The challenge

Health advice teens actually want to read.

Hydration and nutrition affect focus and mood, but advice about them is easy to ignore, especially for teenagers who already find school hard. The idea: turn healthy habits into a game with a comic-book world.

What I did

A comic world with daily goals.

  • Designed the app in Figma and prototyped it in ProtoPie
  • Created the game mechanics: daily challenges, journeys and a garden that grows with good habits
  • Coded and launched the marketing landing page in HTML, CSS and JavaScript
The result

A complete app concept and a live website you can visit today.

Hospital Volunteer App

04
  • ContextExchange program in Shanghai
  • My roleInteractive prototype in ProtoPie
  • ToolsProtoPie · Figma
  • AudienceChinese-speaking hospitals & volunteers

The challenge

Organising volunteers, and keeping patients company.

Hospitals rely on volunteers but struggle to organise them, and many elderly patients spend long days alone. Our international team set out to design an app that solves both problems at once, for a Chinese-speaking audience.

What I did

“Adopt a Grandparent”.

  • Built the interactive prototype in ProtoPie so the team could test the full flow
  • Prototyped the team's standout feature, “Adopt a Grandparent”, which pairs volunteers with elderly patients
  • Designed for a language and culture different from my own, working closely with the team
The result

A clickable prototype you can try yourself, and lots of practice designing across languages and cultures.
